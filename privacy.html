Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026. 502ai, at 502ai.pro. Contact: info@502ai.pro.

What we collect

  • Consultation requests. Your name and email, and any phone number, business name, or note you choose to add.
  • Free IT readiness checklist. Your email address, when you ask for the checklist.
  • Purchases. Stripe processes payments. Stripe gives us your name, email, and what you bought. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Order portal. The private link in your order email, the answers you type into the intake form, and download links we place on that page. Version 1 does not accept file uploads. Please send logos or photos as a Drive link or by email.
  • Support email. Whatever you include when you write to us.

How we use it

We use this information to answer you, deliver what you bought, send order messages, and provide support. We send the free checklist only when you ask for it. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run 502ai, including Stripe for payments and the email and hosting tools we use to deliver an order. We may also share information when the law requires it. A full provider list will be added when those tools are confirmed.

Please do not send sensitive data

Do not include passwords, access codes, payment card numbers, or sensitive records in forms, chat, or email.

Cookies and analytics

We do not run analytics or advertising cookies on 502ai.pro. The order portal is not indexed and does not load third-party scripts.

Marketing email

Order and account messages are not marketing. We send a marketing email only when you have asked for it, or where the law allows, and those messages include a way to opt out.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and order information for as long as we need it to deliver the work, meet tax record rules, and handle a support question. A specific retention period is not published yet. You can ask us to delete your information by emailing info@502ai.pro.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by writing to info@502ai.pro. We will respond. We do not promise a specific number of days.

Children

502ai is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
